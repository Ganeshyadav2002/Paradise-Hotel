Room Types

Single Occpancy Room

A room assigned to one person. It may have one or more beds. The size of a Single Room typically ranges form 37 m² to 45 m².

Family Room

A family room is a living area connected to one or more bedrooms, often with a separate living space. The size of Suite rooms generally ranges form 70 m² to 100 m².

Duplex Room

The most luxurious room in a hotel, typically only one Duplex suite is available per property. Like regular suites, a Duplex suite includes one or more bedrooms and a living area, but with a focus on lavish décor, premium amenities, and personalized services (e.g., a personal butler during the stay).
